Floccus for iPhone and iPad is available from

- [Apple App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/floccus/id1626998357)

Like Floccus in the browser, Floccus for iOS allows you to create and manage multiple profiles.
For technical reasons, the App does not sync your bookmarks with Safari or other mobile browsers,
but makes them available directly in the app itself instead.
You can share links from Safari or other apps to the Floccus App for bookmarking, create and browse folders and search like you would in your browser and open links in your favorite browser.
